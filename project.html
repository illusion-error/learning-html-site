PROJECT IDEAS

Small ideas for useful web projects.

Each idea begins with a clear audience and one useful task. They are deliberately small enough to build, test and improve.

01

Anime Tonight Guide

A spoiler-free guide for viewers who know their mood but not what to watch. Visitors can browse a small set of anime by tone, read short introductions and save a title for later.

For
Anime viewers choosing their next series.
Could include
Cards, filters, accessible buttons and local storage.

02

Campus Quiet Map

A simple guide to calm places for reading, studying and taking a break. Each location has practical details such as noise level, seating, opening hours and the best time to visit.

For
Students looking for a dependable study space.
Could include
Location cards, category filters and a responsive map image.

03

Recipe Timer Notebook

A beginner-friendly cooking notebook for one or two people. Every recipe keeps the ingredient list short and explains one timed task at a time instead of assuming kitchen experience.

For
New cooks preparing simple meals at home.
Could include
Recipe steps, ingredient checklists and kitchen timers.

04

Daily Habit Check-in

A private one-minute page for noticing one habit each day. A reader chooses a habit, records a short reflection and sees a small visual record of recent check-ins.

For
People building one sustainable daily routine.
Could include
A form, clear feedback and browser-based saved entries.

05

Local Walk Planner

A collection of short walks for an unhurried afternoon. Each route explains distance, expected time, nearby landmarks and what makes the walk worth taking.

For
People who want a gentle way to explore nearby places.
Could include
Route cards, distance tags and a weather-aware packing list.
